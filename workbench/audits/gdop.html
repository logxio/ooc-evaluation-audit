What these records measure

The source tracks (S)-10-hydroxycamptothecin in tumor organoids. Diameter is in μm. Grayscale is an intensity from 0 to 255. Day runs from D0 through D4. Each dose and day has 24 channel and chamber readings.

The summary averages 24 technical readings at each of 35 dose and day combinations. The small example table follows channel 1, chamber 1 for each combination. The published endpoint viability is a group-level measure and is not attached to each trajectory point.
