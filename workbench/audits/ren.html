What these records measure

The table contains one paired gemcitabine and cisplatin readout per patient. Smaller AUC means stronger observed in vitro drug effect on the source scale.

The source study reports drug readouts for 54 patients and clinical follow-up for 13. The local paired-drug extract contains 48 patients. A separate frozen clinical audit has 7 strictly evaluable follow-up cases. No individual clinical outcome is assigned to these 48 rows without an ID match.
