What these records measure

Figure 5 source data give cell viability as a fraction relative to the untreated control, which is 1. Concentration is in μM and the SD is on the same viability scale.

The 120 points are repeated dose observations across 20 patient and drug combinations. They are not 120 patients. The sheet supplies no one-to-one patient clinical outcome key for this table.
