# Human 3D Dose Response Corpus

The portable master table contains 13,780 curves and 208,824 measured points. Each row retains its study DOI, biological origin, system type, positive concentration count, observation count and eligibility flags. Stable donor group aliases preserve the resolved equivalence classes. Whole study partitions supply the grouping unit for unresolved donor identities.
